# Code of Conduct 

## Our Commitment
We are committed to creating a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and kind to everyone
- Help beginners and learners
- Use simple and clear language
- Accept constructive feedback

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or rude comments
- Insulting or disrespecting others
- Spam or irrelevant messages
- Toxic behavior of any kind


## Purpose
This project is created for learning and collaboration in open source.
Let’s keep the community positive and helpful
